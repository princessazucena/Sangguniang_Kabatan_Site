About Us

The Sangguniang Kabataan ng Barangay Bukal is the official youth organization that partners with the barangay government to promote the welfare, rights, and development of the youth in the community. As a youth government organization, our goal is to be the voice of the youth and champion programs, projects, and activities focused on education, health, sports, leadership, the environment, and forming responsible citizens. The Sangguniang Kabataan was established to give the youth a chance to take part in governance and decision-making for the betterment of their community.

We believe the youth are the key to a more progressive and brighter future. Through unity, active participation, and meaningful service, we continue to push initiatives that inspire and create opportunities for every young person from Bukal.

As your youth leaders, we are committed to creating programs that bring a positive impact to everyone's life, while upholding transparency, inclusivity, and good governance. Our goal is to be the bridge between the youth and the local government so that their voices, needs, and aspirations are heard and acted on.

"Youth of Bukal: United, Engaged, and Leading the Way to a More Progressive Future."
